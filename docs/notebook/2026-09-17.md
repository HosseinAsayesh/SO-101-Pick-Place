## Problems & how I solved them
- My first "pan keeps radius constant" test failed. The pan axis is at x = +3.9 cm, not at the
  origin. Fixed by measuring radius from the joint anchor. **Lesson: always check where an axis
  actually is.**
- Pan axis points −z, so positive pan = clockwise from above. Easy to get a sign wrong later.
- `wrist_roll` moves the tip ~11 mm at 90° because the `gripperframe` site is ~8 mm off the roll
  axis.

## What I learned
- Serial chain DOF = number of 1-DOF joints; the gripper isn't arm DOF.
- Top-down grasp needs x, y, z, yaw (pitch fixed) → 5 DOF minimum; wrist roll provides yaw.
- 3 parallel pitch joints = planar 3R arm → IK will reduce to 2D geometry.
